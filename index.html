About Me

Crafting digital experiences
one pixel at a time

I'm Arun, a Senior Web Developer and Project Manager based in Kerala, India, with over 15 years of experience delivering high-quality web solutions across a wide spectrum of platforms and industries.

From pixel-perfect HTML/CSS builds to complex API integrations, I bring a meticulous eye for detail, a passion for clean code, and the project management skills to see every build through from brief to launch. I've delivered 200+ websites — from e-commerce stores to corporate portals — all optimised for performance, accessibility, and search.

Over the past year, I have integrated advanced AI engineering workflows (using tools like Antigravity and Codex) into my development lifecycle. This allows me to scaffold modern architecture, implement cutting-edge platform updates across WordPress, Shopify, and Webflow, and deliver complex web solutions at significantly higher velocity—while reserving manual, hands-on craftsmanship for pixel-perfect finishing, bespoke UX logic, and strict WCAG accessibility.

Currently freelancing and open to senior roles internationally, where I can bring my broad skill set to a forward-thinking team.
